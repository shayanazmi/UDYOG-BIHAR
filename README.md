## 📌 Abstract

Bihar stands at a pivotal demographic crossroad. While possessing one of the youngest workforces in India, the state grapples with persistent out-migration (*Palayan*), driven not by aspirational mobility, but by acute local underemployment and wage depression.

This project examines person- and household-level microdata from the **Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) 2025** to diagnose the structural bottlenecks in Bihar's economy and proposes **Project Udyog Bihar**—an evidence-based policy framework to establish local industrial dignity and retain human capital.

---

## 🔍 Key Empirical Insights

| Dimension | Analytical Finding (PLFS 2025) |
|---|---|
| **Wage Divergence** | Regular non-farm wage earners in destination states (Maharashtra, Gujarat, Delhi-NCR) earn significantly higher real daily wages compared to identical skill cohorts within Bihar. |
| **The "Broken Ladder"** | Over 60% of the rural workforce remains trapped in subsistence agriculture and informal casual labor, with an acute deficit in middle-tier formal manufacturing. |
| **Distress Out-Migration** | Overwhelmingly concentrated among youth (aged 18–35), representing a massive drain of productive human capital and local multiplier effects. |
| **Gender Labor Deficit** | Low non-farm Female Labour Force Participation Rate (FLFPR) due to lack of localized, safe, and organized light-manufacturing clusters. |

---

## 🏛️ The 4 Strategic Policy Pillars

1. **Industrial Anchors & BIADA Revitalization**: Fast-tracking plugged-in plug-and-play industrial estates and single-window statutory approvals.
2. **Agro-Processing & Value-Chain Capture**: Scaling industrial-grade processing for high-yield regional strengths (Makhana, Maize, Banana, and Dairy).
3. **MSME Cluster Formalization**: De-risking working capital credit through targeted state guarantee backstops and shared tooling facilities.
4. **Demand-Driven Skill Corridors**: Modernizing ITIs and polytechnic institutes aligned directly with domestic manufacturing and logistics hubs.
